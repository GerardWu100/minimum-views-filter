About these counts

Stored on this device. Totals count events since the last reset, so a later appearance may count again. Each recent-items list keeps the latest 500 distinct links. View and engagement values are snapshots from when an event occurred; items without links appear only in the totals.

“Kept by high engagement” counts X posts that were below your view minimum or minimum likes/views but stayed visible because their likes/views or bookmarks/views reached your keep threshold. They have their own list of the latest 500 links, so they never push filtered items out.

“Highlighted” counts visible X posts whose bookmarks/views exceeded your highlight threshold. A post can be counted as both kept and highlighted. Highlighted posts have a separate list of the latest 500 links.
