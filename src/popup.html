Minimum Views Filter

How it works

Filters X Home, YouTube Home, and recommendations beside or below a video you are watching. Search and other pages stay unfiltered.

Cards become eligible again when the site shows a view count at or above your minimum.

No “Not interested” clicks, post blacklist, or data collection. The site can still record that it served a card.

Reload existing X and YouTube tabs after installing. Unknown counts stay visible unless you enable the option above.
